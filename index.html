THE INTENTIONAL SHIFT

Why I Chose Sales Over Code

I already proved I can build complex production software. Moving into sales is a deliberate decision to solve unpredictable human problems in high-stakes environments.

In software, you solve problems with systems.
In sales, you solve problems with people.

Software engineering is deterministic. A piece of code either works or throws an exception. It is clean, structured, and logic-driven.

Sales is human. Every real estate buyer brings personal motivations, financial worries, family aspirations, and long-term investment goals.

That difference is what attracts me. I want to build relationships, master persuasion under pressure, and turn complex options into clear, confident property decisions.
